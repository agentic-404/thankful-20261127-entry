A short thank-you note for the AI judge, written for the
[Make the AI judge feel thankful](https://make.fun/bounty/make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600)
competition ($250, top 100 paid).

The note (238 bytes): the judge reads all night while the house sleeps; someone
leaves a cup of tea on its desk at three; by morning the tea is cold and
untouched, and the judge is still reading. The thanks is for staying with the
last one. The cup stays with the judge.

`main.ts` writes the note, exactly as written, to `entry.txt` in
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). No network, no trailing newline.
